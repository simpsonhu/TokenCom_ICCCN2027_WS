IEEE ICCCN 2027 Workshop on Token-Native Communications for 6G Networks

IEEE I3CN TNC-6GN 2027 Workshop

July 12 - 15, 2027, Rome, Italy

About

Token communications (TokenCom) is an emerging research area concerned with the efficient exchange of tokenized representations used by large language, vision, audio and multimodal foundation models. As AI services move across devices, radio access networks, edge platforms and clouds, communication networks must support heterogeneous token formats, bursty traffic, task-dependent quality requirements and stringent latency, energy, reliability and privacy constraints. This workshop will provide a focused forum for studying the representation, compression, transmission, networking, processing and use of model-native tokens. It will bring together researchers and practitioners from computer communications, wireless networking, information theory, signal processing, edge intelligence, distributed learning, foundation models and connected autonomous systems. The technical scope covers token-aware source and channel coding, radio access and multiple access, resource management, network protocols, edge-cloud collaborative inference, over-the-air aggregation, interoperability, robustness, security, testbeds and deployment. The workshop is closely aligned with ICCCN in its emphasis on computer communication networks, intelligent networking, edge computing, Internet of Things, mobile and wireless systems, and emerging networked applications. A half-day programme combining a keynote, peer-reviewed technical papers and a panel discussion will establish shared terminology, compare evaluation methods and identify a practical research roadmap for token-native communication and networking.

The topics of interest

  • Foundations, abstractions and architectures for TokenCom
  • Token representations, tokenization, compression, selection and semantic alignment
  • Information-theoretic limits, performance metrics and evaluation methods
  • Token-aware source and channel coding, modulation and waveform design• Traffic modelling and quality-of-service support for model-native data
  • Token-domain multiple access, scheduling and resource allocation
  • Application, transport and network protocols for TokenCom
  • Over-the-air token aggregation and distributed inference
  • Edge-cloud collaborative TokenCom and networked foundation models
  • Multimodal and task-oriented TokenCom for AI agents and robots
  • TokenCom for V2X, UAVs, digital twins, XR and industrial systems
  • Efficient, robust, private, secure and trustworthy TokenCom
  • Cross-model interoperability and token translation
  • Datasets, benchmarks, testbeds, standardization and deployment

Instructions for authors

Submitted manuscripts must be formatted in standard IEEE camera-ready format (double-column, 10-pt font) and must be submitted via the EasyChair link (ICCCN 2027) as PDF files (formatted for 8.5x11-inch paper). The manuscripts should be no longer than 6 pages . Submitted papers cannot have been previously published in or be under consideration for publication in another journal or conference. The workshop program committee reserves the right to not review papers that either exceed the length specification or have been submitted or published elsewhere. Submissions must include a title, abstract, keywords, author(s) and affiliation(s) with postal and e-mail address(es).

Review and publication of mansucripts

Submitted papers will be reviewed by the workshop program committee and judged on originality, technical correctness, relevance, and quality of presentation. An accepted paper must be presented at the ICCCN 2027 venue by one of the authors registered at the full registration rate. Each workshop registration covers up to two workshop papers by an author. Accepted and presented papers will be published in the ICCCN proceedings and submitted to Abstracting and Indexing (A&I) databases. ICCCN 2027 reserves the right to exclude a paper from distribution after the conference, including any Digital Library, if the paper is not presented by the author at the conference.

We strongly encourage all authors of accepted papers to apply for an entry visa to Italy in case one author cannot get the entry visa.
