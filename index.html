IEEE ICCCN 2027 Workshop on Token-Native Communications for 6G Networks

IEEE I3CN TNC-6GN 2027 Workshop

July 12 - 15, 2027, Rome, Italy

About

Token communications (TokenCom) is an emerging research area concerned with the efficient exchange of tokenized representations used by large language, vision, audio and multimodal foundation models. As AI services move across devices, radio access networks, edge platforms and clouds, communication networks must support heterogeneous token formats, bursty traffic, task-dependent quality requirements and stringent latency, energy, reliability and privacy constraints. This workshop will provide a focused forum for studying the representation, compression, transmission, networking, processing and use of model-native tokens. It will bring together researchers and practitioners from computer communications, wireless networking, information theory, signal processing, edge intelligence, distributed learning, foundation models and connected autonomous systems. The technical scope covers token-aware source and channel coding, radio access and multiple access, resource management, network protocols, edge-cloud collaborative inference, over-the-air aggregation, interoperability, robustness, security, testbeds and deployment. The workshop is closely aligned with ICCCN in its emphasis on computer communication networks, intelligent networking, edge computing, Internet of Things, mobile and wireless systems, and emerging networked applications. A half-day programme combining a keynote, peer-reviewed technical papers and a panel discussion will establish shared terminology, compare evaluation methods and identify a practical research roadmap for token-native communication and networking.
